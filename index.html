Career highlights

At Google, I move teams, data, and tools through company deals

I am a Technical Program Manager at Google in the San Francisco Bay Area. I lead technical execution for mergers, acquisitions, and divestitures across Google and Alphabet. I partner with software engineering, security, and product teams to build data migration platforms, automation pipelines, and productivity workflows.

  • Migrations

    I led the design and rollout of new pipelines that move issue tracker and team wiki data into Google tools.

  • Onboarding

    I set a repeatable plan for acquired technical teams to move their critical workflows to secure Google workstations.

  • Cleanup

    I led technical planning to audit and retire legacy cloud domains left over from past deals.

  • Automation

    I built a production Python service backed by Cloud Spanner that tracks engineering time across closed work items.

  • Brought structured logging, error handling, and monitoring to migration tools, and wrote privacy design documents for them.
  • Added AI and LLM tools to technical onboarding for acquired teams.
  • Mapped the systems of acquired companies in AI, cloud, and security, and routed their work to Google infrastructure.
Research

Three papers from my years in food science

Before Google, I studied integrated sciences at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln and worked as a research assistant in its Food Science Department. That work led to three papers and one conference abstract on machine learning, Raman spectroscopy, and plant-based food. I then earned a master of science at the University of Notre Dame.
